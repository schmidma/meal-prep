# Coloured-pencil meal illustrations

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Approved direction: medium-detail coloured pencil, warm natural colours, gentle shading and light background. App assets are optimized WebP files in `static/images/illustrations/`.

## Prompt template

Create a standalone landscape 4:3 food illustration for a meal-planning app. Subject: SUBJECT. Use warm natural colors and a colored-pencil technique, visible fine dry cross-hatching, gently irregular pencil contours, medium detail, light shading. Clearly a drawn colored-pencil cookbook illustration; no watercolor, no gouache, no photorealism, no glossy rendering. Warm tomato red, olive green, golden bread and ivory colors as appropriate to the food, moderate saturation. One centered vignette, slight elevated three-quarter view, complete plate or bowl visible, occupying about 65-70 percent of canvas width with generous plain off-white margins. Clean light background, no decorative scenery, no text, no labels, no letters, no border or watermark. Keep food forms recognizable at thumbnail size and do not over-detail. Vegetarian food only. Output ONE individual image, not a comparison grid.
